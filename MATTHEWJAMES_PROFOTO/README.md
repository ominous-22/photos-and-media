## About

This collection explores the intersection of urban decay and human presence,
capturing the raw beauty found in Portland's industrial landscapes and forgotten
spaces. Through high-contrast black and white imagery interspersed with moments
of color, each photograph reveals the textures, patterns, and quiet poetry of
the everyday urban experience.

22 photographs + 1 mixed media composition.
